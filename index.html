How this is built: a "sale" is a policy keyed row from the uploaded production file, restricted to the trailing 60 days () and matched against the active ACP/AFP roster () — Regional Sales Directors, Field Sales Managers, and Sales Office staff are excluded, and anyone keying sales who isn't on that advisor roster is dropped from this view. Days since last sale = As-of date − most recent Keyed Date within the window, per advisor. Red = 30+ days, Yellow = 15–29 days, Green = 0–14 days. The trend line under each advisor shows total original sales amount keyed per week across the 60-day window. Because the window is fixed to policies keyed in the last 60 days, any advisor with zero sales in that stretch does not appear on the board above — this view shows recent sellers only, not the full roster. The Gone Dark panel below (click to expand) covers that gap: it lists every active-roster advisor with no sale in the last 60 days, with their most recent sale pulled from the full 2026 file where one exists. Advisors tagged "No 2026 sales on record" have no matching keyed sale anywhere in the uploaded file this year — likely a recent hire, an extended leave, a departure from Forest Lawn not yet reflected in this roster, or (less likely) a name that didn't match cleanly between the roster and the production file.
